# 🎬 EPISODE 1 — “WRONG TIME TO DIE”

---

## 🎞️ OPENING FRAME

* Film flicker
* Dust scratches
* Piano starts: slow, tired melody

### **[IRIS IN]**

Reveal:

* MC sitting in a creaky chair
* Slight wobble in his whole body

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“After a lifetime of work…”**

---

## 🎞️ SCENE 1 — HIS APARTMENT

* He stands up
  → spine squiggles
  → knees wobble late

* Reaches for wallet
  → drops it
  → stares at it… sighs… slowly bends

**Visual gag:**
His back bends in 3 separate delayed segments

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“…he had finally reached retirement.”**

---

* He opens wallet:

  * A few bills
  * A small note: “Budget carefully.”

* He imagines (thought bubble):

  * Beach
  * Travel
  * Relaxing

**SMASH CUT back to reality:**
His knee pops loudly (“POP” appears on screen)

---

## 🎞️ SCENE 2 — HEADING OUTSIDE

* He exits building

* Bright light → he squints dramatically

* Starts walking:

  * Cane taps rhythmically
  * Body slightly desynced

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“A simple trip to the store.”**

---

## 🚛 SCENE 3 — TRUCK-KUN INTRO

### First Hint

* Background: far distance
* A truck… barely visible
* Slight unnatural wobble

---

### MC walking (foreground)

* Stops to stretch back
* Takes *way too long*

---

### Cut back:

Truck is closer now

* Engine rattles unnaturally
* Headlights flicker like eyes

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“…unfortunately, he was not alone.”**

---

## 🎞️ STALKING SEQUENCE (COMEDY BUILD)

* MC ties shoe slowly
  → truck creeps closer in background

* MC checks grocery list
  → truck rolls forward slightly

* MC pauses to rest against pole
  → truck idles… waiting

**Running gag:**
Truck always stops when he looks up

---

## 🎞️ CLOSE-UP — TRUCK

* Front grill bends slightly (like a grin)
* Headlights narrow

---

## 🎞️ SCENE 4 — CROSSWALK

* MC arrives at street
* Looks left
* Looks right

Takes a *long pause* because his neck hurts

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“He checked carefully.”**

---

* Looks left again (extra slow)

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“…perhaps too carefully.”**

---

## 🎞️ THE HIT (COMEDIC TIMING)

* He finally steps forward

**PAUSE**

**SMASH CUT:**

Truck instantly fills frame
(no buildup now—just sudden chaos)

* Impact happens OFFSCREEN (keep it stylized)

---

## 🎞️ VISUAL GAG

* His cane lands upright
  …then slowly falls over

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“Retirement was… brief.”**

---

## ✨ SCENE 5 — SUMMONING

### 🎞️ HARD CUT

* Bright white circle
* Smooth, perfect magic lines (no wobble)

**Contrast hits immediately**

---

* Figures in robes stand around
* Everything is elegant, stable

---

## 🎞️ SUMMONING EFFECT

* Light pulses smoothly
* Energy flows beautifully

Then—

**POP**

He appears:

* Crooked posture
* Slightly off-balance
* Cane half-materializes late

---

## 🎞️ SILENCE BEAT

Everyone stares

He squints at them

---

## 🎴 INTERTITLE

**“...This is not the store.”**

---

## 🎞️ REACTION SHOTS

### Summoners:

* Shocked
* Confused
* Whispering dramatically

### Him:

* Adjusts back
* Winces
* Looks around unimpressed

---

## 🎞️ FINAL GAG

* He tries to stand straight heroically

→ spine straightens for half a second
→ LOUD “CRACK” text appears
→ immediately slouches again

---

## 🎴 FINAL INTERTITLE

**“The hero had arrived.”**

---

## 🎞️ LAST SHOT

* Wide shot:

  * Elegant summoning circle
  * Dramatic robes
  * Glowing magic

* Center:

  * One tired, crooked man

---

## 🎞️ IRIS OUT

Piano ends on a slightly off-key note

---

## 🎯 WHY THIS WORKS

* Truck-kun becomes a **character**, not just a trope
* Silent film pacing makes the hit funnier, not darker
* Immediate tone established:

  * World = dramatic
  * MC = unimpressed and uncomfortable
