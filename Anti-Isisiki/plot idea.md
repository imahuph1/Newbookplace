# bad plot

50 somthing year old summoned to a new world.  
His poor choices in life on earth lead to bad joints, a sore back, knees that forsee the weather, bad eyesite and a general crochety attitude.
Uppon arriving in the new world the people around the summoning circle are sad to see a man older than their own king within in, using a cane.
Although he has enormus potential to gain great power it requires work, and lots of it, and he has no interest in putting in the work, he is enough pain as it is.

---

## 🎬 Series Concept

**Working Title:** *“Summoned, Sore, and Over It”*

A burnt-out, chronically sore man gets summoned to another world right when he expected to finally rest… only to be given a second life that demands effort. He refuses—at first.

Tone:

* Dry humor, sarcasm
* Absurd fantasy vs mundane complaints
* Gradual emotional shift (not preachy)

Style:

* “Wiggly noodle” animation = loose, shaky lines
* His body literally *wiggles worse* when in pain
* Magic is smooth and fluid → contrasts with his stiff movements

---

## 🧩 Core Theme

> “A second chance only matters if you actually use it.”

---

## 📺 Episode Outline (Short Series: ~8–10 episodes, 5–8 min each)

---

### **Episode 1 – “Wrong Time to Die”**

* Opens on his last day on Earth:

  * Counting retirement money
  * Complaining about knees, back, eyes
  * Mentions trips he *wanted* to take
* Dies suddenly (keep it quick, not heavy)
* Smash cut → summoning circle

**Punchline:**
He looks around and says:

> “Can I go back? I just got off work.”

---

### **Episode 2 – “This Is Not an Upgrade”**

* Fantasy people expect a hero
* He can barely stand without his cane
* They explain: “You have immense potential!”

**His response:**

> “I had potential 30 years ago. Look where that got me.”

* Refuses training immediately

---

### **Episode 3 – “Level 1 Pain”**

* Learns:

  * Stats exist
  * Training = literal improvement to body
* Attempts one basic action → pulls something instantly

**Visual gag:**

* Level-up glow starts… then fizzles because he quits mid-effort

---

### **Episode 4 – “Retirement 2.0”**

* Tries to recreate a lazy life:

  * Finds a chair
  * Avoids everyone
  * Complains about fantasy food

But:

* Pain doesn’t go away
* No healthcare, no safety net

**Ending beat:**
He realizes:

> “So I still have to work… just to not hurt?”

---

### **Episode 5 – “Physical Therapy Arc”**

* Reframes training as:

  * Stretching
  * Light movement
  * Small, manageable effort

**Tone shift:**

* Still grumpy, but slightly more engaged

**Visual:**

* His “wiggly” lines stabilize slightly after tiny improvements

---

### **Episode 6 – “Kids With Bad Ideas”**

* Meets younger adventurers rushing into danger
* Sees his past self in them (overworking, bad choices)

He gives lazy, blunt advice:

> “You keep that up, your knees are gonna sound like gravel.”

They accidentally take him seriously

---

### **Episode 7 – “Not a Hero, Just… Less Miserable”**

* Gains small abilities:

  * Better balance
  * Slightly less pain
* Saves someone *by accident* (low effort, clever thinking)

People start calling him wise

**He hates this.**

---

### **Episode 8 – “What Was the Point?”**

* Quiet episode
* He reflects on Earth:

  * Worked nonstop
  * Saved just enough
  * Never lived

**Key realization:**

> “I didn’t ruin my body… I just never used it for anything I wanted.”

---

### **Episode 9 – “Try, But Not Too Hard”**

* He *chooses* to try—not for glory
* Sets his own rules:

  * No overworking
  * No chasing power for its own sake

**Goal becomes:**

> Move without pain, live a little

---

### **Episode 10 – “A Small, Stubborn Life” (Finale)**

* Not a grand hero ending
* He:

  * Walks without a cane (sometimes)
  * Travels short distances
  * Still complains constantly

Final line:

> “Still hurts… but not as much.”

**Last shot:**
He watches a sunset—something he never had time for before

---

## 🧠 Character Arc (Simple)

* Start: “I’m done trying.”
* Middle: “I’ll try a little, just to feel less awful.”
* End: “I’ll try, because I actually want something now.”

---

## 🎨 Animation-Specific Gags (Important for your style)

* His joints *creak visually* (like bending noodles with sound effects)
* When he overdoes it, his body spaghetti-collapses
* Magic = smooth, flowing lines (contrast with his jittery body)
* Level-up UI flickers like it’s annoyed with him quitting

---

## 💡 Why this works

* It avoids cliché “chosen one” energy
* The stakes are personal, not world-ending
* Relatable without being heavy
* Comedy carries the slower emotional growth
