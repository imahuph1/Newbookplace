# World rules

## Sect Rules

### Registered Disciples

- three years to reach qi condensation
- no required tasks
- basic porridge for food three times a day
- one low grade spirit stone and one tier one spirit elixir a month
- if you do not make it to qi condensation you will be sent out into the mortal world to handle basic sect duties.

### Outer Disciples

- thirty years to make foundation 
- two tasks required annually
- better food daily
- three low grade spirit stones and two second or third tier spirit elixirs a month.
- if you do not make it to foundation establishment you will be sent out into the mortal world to handle sect duties.
