### Chapter 1 – Awakening

Jimmy Jones knew something had gone wrong the moment the light faded.

Jimmy stared at the empty air in front of him.

Nothing.

No panel.\
No skills.\
No attributes.

Just the same dull system interface he’d had since childhood—wallet balance at zero, storage empty.

Time stopped.

Then a new message appeared, written in cold, indifferent text.

> **Due to this world’s lack of cultivation resources, a supplementary system has been authorized.**

Jimmy’s heart slammed against his ribs.

> **Pay-to-Win System Activated.**

A second window followed.

> **Welcome, Cultivator.**\
> **Would you like to open the Newbie Gift Pack?**

Jimmy didn’t hesitate.

“Yes.”

The moment the word left Jimmy’s mouth, the world seemed to _click_—as if a lock he hadn’t known existed had just been opened.

> **Newbie Gift Pack Opening…**

Light poured into his vision, not blinding, but _dense_. Information followed immediately, flooding his mind with clarity rather than confusion.

> **You have received:**
>
> • **Celestial body cultivation manual**\
> • **Low-Grade Spirit Gathering Pill ×3**\
> • **Tier Three sword**\
> • **100 System Gold**
> • **Dummies Guide to cultivation**

Jimmy’s breath caught.

Gold.

Real, spendable gold—sitting in his system wallet like it had always belonged there.

> **Note:** All cultivation-related items purchased or obtained through the System are compatible with your talent: **Absolute Comprehension**.

He focused on the manual.

**Celestial body cultivation manual**\
_Quality: Devine_\
_Description: Requires years for proper comprehension._

The moment Jimmy selected it, the manual dissolved into motes of light.

There was no chanting.\
No posture.\
No painstaking trial and error.

Understanding simply… _arrived_.

His breathing adjusted on its own.\
His heartbeat slowed into a precise rhythm.\
Something invisible stirred inside his body, flowing through channels that had never existed a second ago.

> **Cultivation Technique Learned.**\
> **Status: Perfected.**

Jimmy’s eyes widened.

Perfected.

Not _learned_. Not _basic mastery_.

Perfected.

A warmth spread from his core outward, settling into his limbs like molten metal cooling into shape.

> **Mortal Foundation Established.**
>
> **Body Refinement Tier 1 Achieved.**

Not a level, no experiance but a strange label.

Jimmy Jones — **body refinement tier 1**

He was staring at a system shop window that absolutely should not exist.

_Talisman patterns_\
_Arrays_\

Jimmy swallowed.

This world didn’t have cultivation resources.

The system did.

And suddenly, the rules everyone else lived by felt… optional.

He closed the shop window, heart racing—not from fear, but from anticipation.

Night was coming there would be no testing what kind of combat this class is able to do.
