# Idea and world rules

## 1. Refined Setting & System Bible

### Protagonist

**Name:** Jimmy Jones\
**Class:** Unique Class – _Sword Cultivator_\
**Talent:** **Absolute Comprehension**

* Any cultivation-related technique, manual, or skill purchased through the **System Store** is automatically raised to **Perfected Level**

* No bottlenecks, no misunderstandings, no failed breakthroughs

***

### Cheat / System

**System Name:** Pay-to-Win System (Supplementary Authorization)

**Core Functions:**

* **System Wallet**

  * Holds gold obtained from selling items, beast cores, monster drops, etc.

  * Gold can be used directly in the System Shop for:

    * Cultivation techniques

    * Pills

    * Manuals

    * Resources unavailable in this world

* **System Storage**

  * Infinite storage, time-stasis

  * All trades and item transfers occur through the system

* **Exchange Function**

  * Items considered “useless” or low-value in this world (beast cores, monster materials, otherworldly drops) can be sold at **real-world equivalent exchanges** through the system

  * Converts directly into system gold

light, mild comedy

### Awakening Day

on their **sixteenth**, all humans awaken a class.

* Even the lowest classes (e.g., Farmer) receive:

  * Skills

  * Attribute panels

  * Growth paths

**Jimmy’s Awakening:**

* Receives **Sword Cultivator** class

* No skills

* No attributes

* No explanations

* Only the basic system interface he’s always had: wallet + storage

Then:

> **Due to this world’s lack of cultivation resources, a supplementary system has been authorized.**\
> **Pay-to-Win System Activated.**
>
> **Would you like to open the Newbie Gift Pack?**

***

### World Rules

#### Levels

* **Level Cap:** 1–100

* EXP gained by killing:

  * Beasts

  * Monsters

  * Otherworldly beings

**Progression:**

* Levels 1–10: relatively easy

* Difficulty increases exponentially

* Every **10 levels**, a mandatory quest must be completed to break through

* Breakthroughs unlock:

  * New skills

  * Higher-tier abilities

**Lifespan:**

* Increases with level

* Up to **10,000 years** at higher levels

***

#### Dungeons

* Can be entered **once per day**

* If fully cleared:

  * Dungeon can be rerun at higher difficulty

* Drop rates:

  * Field monsters: low

  * Dungeon clears: decent to high

***

#### Night Rule (Absolute Law)

* **Never be away from a temple at night**

* If you are:

  * A non-physical entity will hunt and kill you

  * No known method of resistance

  * Cannot be fought, killed, or escaped

***

#### Talents

* Not all awakened possess talents

* Talents synergize with class

  * Example: Blacksmith + Talent → better refinement, higher weapon tiers with same materials

* Jimmy’s talent synergizes with cultivation **to an absurd degree**
