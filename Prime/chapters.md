John Smith was until very recently about to retire.  In fact just this morning he filed his paperwork, in two weeks he no longer had to get up in the morning to go to work.  He could sleep in until he wanted to wake up, do whatever he feels like doing, and go to bed as late as he wants.  That was the plan, it was a good plan, the best laid plans never survive the first encounter with the enemy.  
Laying in bed, making plans to hit his favorite fishing spots, what new rod to buy, learning to tie flys, all the fun things he has been wanting to do for years.  Everything was looking good until the ticking time bomb in his head went off.  Brain aneurism something nobody looks for and there is not much they can do for them even if they are caught.  John would not be seeing his retirement, heck he won't even see tomorrow morning.

John woke up, the first thing he noticed was that he was not dead, must have been a bad dream.  Why his bed was so uncomfortable he did not know.  He opened his eyes and noticed that the ceiling was not white, it was wood planks, not even stained, just raw wood.  For several seconds he just lay there on an uncomfortable bed staring at the un-familiar ceiling.

Perhaps he thought this is the dream, he closed his eyes again.  Then it came all at once, memories that were not his.  A sickly child who spend his life on a small farm.  A father who worked the fields.  A mother who handled the house.  A world where spiritual energy existed, beasts that grow powerful enough to destroy entire regions.  A world where cultivation is not a myth.

His parents died, they were taking the surplus to market.  Normally the roads are well patrolled and safe, but a beast slipped through and the caravan his parents where in was attacked.  The city guard informed him this morning.  He knows he is more or less safe in this house for a little while.  Only fourteen but in a world of cultivators a young man like him should be able to work the farm, as long as the farm produces and he makes yearly rent and taxes the city lord wont boot him out.  Should he not make rent he will be forcibly evicted.  

"I was supposed to retire." He slowly lowered hid head into his hands.  He had spend fifty years working all for the chance to enjoy his golden years. Now he is expected to start over again, physical labor, work the land.  This body is ill suited for such work.

Just as he was beginning to panic a voice sounded in his head.

'*Host conciseness stabilized.*'
'*Memories synced, reincarnation successful.*'
'*Idle Cultivation System Version a0.01 has completed binding.*'

John's head jerked up, "A system, seriously a system, I thought thats was only a bad trope in pulp online novels"

'*Welcome, Host.  Rest assured this is not a novel*'

"Not a novel huh, I died, I am reborn on a cultivation world as an orphan, and I am bonded with a system.  How is this not a novel."
