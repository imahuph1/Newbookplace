# Chapter 1 
## Awakening

Jimmy Jones knew something had gone wrong the moment the light faded.

He stared at the empty air in front of him.

Nothing.

No panel.\
No skills.\
No attributes.

Just the same dull system interface he’d had since childhood—wallet balance at zero, storage empty.

Around him, the Awakening Temple buzzed with noise. Cheers, gasps, the occasional shout of excitement. Panels flashed into existence in front of other sixteen-year-olds, glowing with classes and skills that would define the rest of their lives.

Jimmy’s had… not.

Time seemed to stretch.

Then a new message appeared, written in cold, indifferent text.

> **Due to this world’s lack of cultivation resources, a supplementary system has been authorized.**

Jimmy’s heart slammed against his ribs.

> **Pay-to-Win System Activated.**

A second window followed immediately.

> **Welcome, Sword Cultivator.**\
> **Primary Combat Path Detected.**\
> **Would you like to open the Newbie Gift Pack?**

Jimmy didn’t hesitate.

“Yes.”

The moment the word left his mouth, the world seemed to _click_—as if a lock he hadn’t known existed had just been opened.

> **Newbie Gift Pack Opening…**

Light poured into his vision. Not blinding, but dense. Heavy with meaning.

Information followed, flooding his mind—not chaotically, but with unnatural clarity.

> **You have received:**
>
> • **Celestial Body Cultivation Manual**\
> • **Body Refinement Pill ×3**\
> • **Tier Three Sword**\
> • **Nine Swords Technique**\
> • **100 Gold**\
> • **Cultivation for Dummies**

Jimmy’s breath caught.

Gold.

Real, spendable gold—sitting in his system wallet like it had always belonged there.

> **Note:** All cultivation-related items purchased or obtained through the System are compatible with your talent: **Absolute Comprehension**.

Talent.

That, at least, he had.

He focused on the first item.

**Celestial Body Cultivation Manual**\
_Quality: Divine_\
_Description: Requires years for proper comprehension._

The moment Jimmy selected it, the manual dissolved into motes of light.

There was no chanting.\
No posture.\
No painstaking trial and error.

Understanding simply… _arrived_.

His breathing adjusted on its own.\
His heartbeat slowed, settling into a precise rhythm.\
Something invisible stirred inside his body, flowing through channels that had not existed a second ago.

The technique required no additional thought or action from him.\
Every breath seemed to draw something ephemeral inward, refining it without resistance.

> **Cultivation Technique Learned.**\
> **Status: Perfected.**

Jimmy’s eyes widened.

Perfected.

Not _learned_.\
Not _level one_.

Perfected, maxed.

Warmth spread from his core, radiating outward, settling into his limbs like molten metal cooling into shape.

> **Mortal Foundation Established.**
>
> **Body Refinement Tier 1 Achieved.**

Not a level.

No experience gained.

Just a strange, absolute label.

Jimmy Jones — **Body Refinement Tier 1**

A new window opened in front of him—one that absolutely should not exist.

### **— SYSTEM SHOP —**

_Sword Techniques_\
_Movement Techniques_\
_Defensive Techniques_\
_Pills & Elixirs_\
_Talisman Patterns_\
_Arrays_\
_Weapons_\
_Resources (Unavailable in This World)_

At the very top, highlighted in gold:

**Spirit Stones (Low-Grade)** — _10 Gold Each_

Jimmy swallowed.

This world didn’t have cultivation resources.

The system did.

And suddenly, the rules everyone else lived by felt… optional.

He closed the shop window slowly, his heart racing—not from fear, but from anticipation.

Night was coming.\
There would be no testing what this class could do today.

He would sit back, stay inside the temple’s safe zone, and read _Cultivation for Dummies_.\
Figure out what cultivation actually meant.\
Why there were no attributes.\
Why there were no levels.

Because whatever this class was—

It didn’t fit the world.

And that, Jimmy suspected, was the point.
