# I Accidentally Awakened as a God and Still Failed Group Projects

## I Think I Did It Wrong

Nemo Nikt is a nobody, he is not entirely certain if it is because that is how his parents named him, if it is just a twist of fate, or if it has something to do with him.  His name literally means nobody nobody. His parents thought it was funny a nobody from a family of nobodies.
He was born to two loving parents who are low level service workers, they barely make enough to live in the poorest part of town.  He attended his compulsory education in the poorest school in the capital city, there is not much hope for anybody here, the vast majority will not awaken a class and return to their families, get a low wage job, and continue to populate the outer slums.
That is all that anyone expects from him.  He is somewhat looking forward to relaxing in a low wage job, doing the bare minimum to not be fired.  Still, it is a requirement that he attend the awakening ceremony, by the gods he hates crowds, they are just so suffocating and noisy.
Still if he wants to graduate, so he can officially be hired at a convenience store as a night clerk, his dream job, he must attend so here he is at the ceremony hiding at the back of the crowd of soon-to-be former students.  Almost everybody here knew Nemo and ignored him, he just was not fun to have a conversation with, he seemed cold, and when asked a question answered literally, Nemo just did not understand the nuance of conversation.
There were a few families here who had high hopes that their children would awaken a class, become professionals, and raise the family out of the slums to a higher social class.  His family did not have the money to purchase the resources to increase the chance of awakening, and Nemo never showed such an interest.  The vast majority of students at this school are either content with their lot in life or are simply resigned to it.
The students all filed into the auditorium, the excited sitting up front and the less excited towards the rear, the families and teachers all sat to one side with the students on the other.  One by one starting from the front row a student went up, pressed his hand on the awakening crystal, occasionally the crystal would flicker, and the monitor would proclaim their new class. The vast majority did not get a class, and the few that did where common classes in the life class category, a good step up from no class, but not the vaulted combat class.
Nemo barely paid any attention to the ceremony as one by one each of the students and their families exited the auditorium.  Until finally there was just him and a few teachers and the monitor left.  Nemo made his way onto the stage and pressed his hand to the awakening stone.  There was a bright flash, the monitor looked surprised, "Class, Assassin, congratulations on awakening a combat class."
