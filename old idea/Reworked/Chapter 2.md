## Dinner and a Restful Night

Dinner was everything Jimmy had imagined—and more.

The prime rib arrived thick-cut and perfectly seared, juices pooling beneath it as steam rose when he sliced into the meat. It was so tender he barely needed the knife. He started with small bites, savoring each one. Flavor exploded across his tongue—salt, fat, heat, richness.

He chewed slowly and deliberately, forcing himself not to rush. He’d learned that lesson the hard way. After weeks of eating the bare minimum to survive, wolfing down real food came with consequences.

The bread had a crisp crust; when he broke it open, the crumb inside was soft and released a puff of steam. The vegetables were cooked just right—tender, with just a bit of bite. There was even a small dessert included.

By the time Jimmy leaned back in his chair, his limbs felt heavy in the best possible way.

Around the dining room, light chatter drifted through the air. Merchants and travelers laughed at shared jokes, enjoying their meals. A few families celebrated sons or daughters who had awakened promising classes earlier that day. No one paid Jimmy any attention, and he paid none to them.

This was a good hotel.

Jimmy planned on staying.

He returned to his room and collapsed onto the bed. The mattress yielded beneath him—soft but supportive. Clean sheets brushed against clean skin. He stared at the ceiling, waiting for the unease to come. Waiting for the moment when the other shoe would drop.

Sleep claimed him before it did.

An hour before dawn, his system alarm woke him.

It was an alarm he’d set years ago—time to get up, get moving, stake his claim.

Jimmy stretched and opened his eyes.

Adrenaline hit him like a freight train.

_Where am I? What happened?_

Panic surged before memories of the previous day filtered back in. The panic faded, replaced by curiosity.

He pulled up his system interface.

> **Name:** Jimmy Jones\
> **Class:** Sword Cultivator (Unique Path: Sword Immortal)\
> **Wallet:** 99 Gold, 0 Silver, 0 Copper\
> **Body Refinement:** Forty Five Percent\
> **Store Access:** Tier One

He swung his legs over the side of the bed. His body moved easily, _too_ easily. There was a lightness to him he’d never known, a quiet strength coiled beneath his skin. When he stood, there was no stiffness, no lingering ache from cold stone or hard ground.

Jimmy exhaled slowly.

This wasn’t imagination. This was real.

The only question was whether it was permanent, or borrowed. The system had given him this. That meant it could take it away just as easily.

He needed to make the most of it while he could.

## First Steps Outside the Walls

Morning light filtered through the city as Jimmy stepped out of the hotel.   the streets were waking up.  Vendors shouted prices, cars where moving along the streets, the smell of fresh bread mingled with the less pleasant odors drifting up from the gutters.  People moved with purpose, workers heading to jobs, merchants opening stalls and shops.  

Jimmy blended in.   That alone was new.   Clean cloths, clean skin, a straight backed posture that came naturally now instead of being forced.  No one looked at him twice.  No one watched him like he might steal something if they turned away.  He was used to being noticed but ignored by the common people, and watched warily by the shopkeepers and vendors, but now he just blended in, unnoticed.
