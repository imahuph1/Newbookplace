# World rules

## Leveling

- experianced is gained through life, one must internalize the experiance to fully take hold of it
- Stats are mind body and soul
- each level up, when internalized experiance reaches one hundred percent gain one stat point
- each level up temperaraly stops ageing one must continue to level to stay 'young'.
- reaching higher levels takes greater and greater effort. 
- each level up solifies growth in each stat, adding the point adds a stacking multplier to the stat


## magic

- magic is common place
- everyone can sense the mana around and within themselves
- some specialize in the ulilization to directly impact the world around them
- most just use it for various daily tasks.
- first level spells 2d simple acts, lighting fires, launching a fire ball, small local breeze
- second level spells 3d more complex acts, teleporting, time manipulation, invisibilty, creating permiminent spells.

## condensed mana and mana shield

- high soul and mind allow gathering mana around the person
- this mana will blunt basic attacks
- this mana becomes the persons mana
- this mana can be used to feed a mana shield spell
- the stronger mind and soul, with experiance can control all the mana in an area
- controlling the mana can be contested by a group or a similarly strong mage
- multiple people with the same goal can control/disrupt the mana in an area

## emotion and psychic fields

- every person has a psychic field, they can control them 
- linked to mind and body stat, experiance and practice can control the feild
- the stronger the experiance and stats the more the field can interact with the people and world around them.
- the world has its own idea of what it wants to be, people animals and other beings can change an area
- spawn points are basicaly the world deciding a certain type of animal should be in this area, the animal being in the area re-inforces the idea it should be there.

## tone

- satirical, 
- poke fun at common fantasy tropes
- poke fun at spiritualizm, personal growth
- MC is dropped into a world where all of this is real, and he has to deal with it.
- MC is introverted, does not like engaging with people, naturaly sarcastic as a defence mechanism
- MC soul found this world, took over a body, due to not being born into this world cannot be prdicted, is outside of fate, every action he takes changes the fate of those around him.
- King, used to being able to see and effect the fate of those around him is both ammused and annoyed by the fact that the MC exists.  So far the ammusement is the overiding factor.  

## skills vs stats

- sword skill at level five hundred means real good with a sword, not that he can cut a mountain in half
- a strength of one hundred means real strong, with ten stars means make diamond out of coal in their fist


## world

- human influcance on this plane is minimal, they are in 'control' of an area the size of the north america
- this is a small fraction of a single continant of the plane.
- the kingdom is a series of city states, each city controls their region, the kingdom sets the main laws, collects taxes, has a strong army.
- primarily a meritocricy, cities have levle limits, based on the mana density of the area.  as cities level up the level limit of the city can be raised.
- low level cities with a low mana density limit the level/complexity of the spells 
- mana density influenced by population and cohesivness
- magitek is highly advanced, everyone is constantly being monitored by an artificial intelligance.  it will flag those who break laws. not quite big brother but close


## laws

- noble wars, assinations, economic wars, everything has a law attached, laws are created and enforced by the government, primarily the king.  the laws where written by the founding king.  
- for the pourpouse of individual growth higher level people are not allowed to interfere with lower level people, they can guide but have to be carefull not to change how they view things.
