# Ideas

## Mana is everywhere

In this world mana is everywhere, where it is more dense the stronger the beasts, the greater the rewards
Everyone uses mana, they do not control it they absorb it and it strengthens them, they absorb it by eating the beasts and vegitables.
MC is reborn in this world, he has the ability to control the mana, the trick is a moderate understanding of modern physics and chemistry.

## Mana absorbsion

Mana is aborbed from fooods and drinks made from beasts and mana rich food items.  Foods made from low density areas have a small amount and will eventualy reach an equilibrium, The higher the desisty mana regions have more mana, and will slowly increase the duriability and streangth of those eating it, if it is the main fair eventualy it will reach equilibrium.  

## Mana Control

While controling the mana in the environment is possible and converting it to elements can be done, most people don't bother.  The elements quickly break back down into mana when the control is lost or cut off.  It requires good understanding of the element and strong concentration to maintain the control of the mana.
MC understands the make up of the elements and basic chemistry, he can with concentration create things like fire, he understands that fire needs oxygen, a fuel source and heat.  With this basic knowlage he can then create more and more powerful, self propigating things, barriors, water, zones of carbon monoxide, ect.
After some time can use mana to examine the atomic makeup of various compounds.  In the beginning simple compounds, as he moves to higher density areas can examine and create more complex compunds.
