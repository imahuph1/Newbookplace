# Introduction

## death

John died as pitifully as he had lived.

A big box truck ended him.

There wasn’t even time to scream. One second he was crossing the street, the next there was the blare of a horn, a wall of steel, and then nothing but the violent sensation of impact.

Darkness swallowed him.

For a long moment there was no pain, no sound, no body.

Only thought.

And unfortunately, John had a lot of those.

Memories came rushing back.

Every little thing.

Every insult.

Every time someone looked at him like he was less than human. The sneers. The dismissive tones. The quiet contempt people had for someone like him.

A nobody.

He remembered the times he had struggled just to keep the lights on while politicians talked about prosperity on television. The times corporations squeezed him for every dollar they could. The time he forgot to pay a tiny bill and ended up sitting in a jail cell for weeks like a criminal.

Meanwhile people who actually ruined lives walked free.

People with money.

People with power.

People with connections.

They could lie, cheat, steal—even kill—and somehow the system bent around them like it was made of rubber.

But John?

John forgot paperwork and the hammer came down.

He clenched his phantom teeth in the void.

Now here he was. Dead in the street because some driver was probably looking at their phone.

The driver might get a fine.

Maybe a slap on the wrist.

Then they'd go home and forget he ever existed.

Murder without consequences.

Just another statistic.

John had always been weak.

No money.

No strength.

No connections.

No one who cared.

Every day of his life he had paid for it.

The dirty looks.

The small humiliations.

The constant reminder that the world belonged to other people.

And now he was gone.

Just like that.

The anger bubbled up again, the same impotent rage that had followed him his entire life.

If only he had power.

If only he had strength.

If only he had the ability to make the world treat him differently.

The thought burned in the empty darkness.

For a moment, nothing happened.

Then the void flickered.

A faint blue light appeared in front of him.

Words formed in the darkness.

---
>
> **SYSTEM INITIALIZING**  
> **SOUL DETECTED**  
> **MEMORY INTEGRITY: STABLE**  
> **PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE: EXTREME RESENTMENT / HIGH SURVIVAL DRIVE**  
> **COMPATIBILITY FOUND**  
> **REINCARNATION PROTOCOL ACTIVATED**
>
---

John blinked.

Or at least he felt like he blinked.

"What?" he said.

His voice echoed in the emptiness.

More text appeared.

---
>
> **REWARD: SECOND LIFE**  
> **CLASS SELECTION COMPLETE**  
> **CLASS: WEAPON SUMMONER**  
> **CORE ABILITY GRANTED: SHADOW SPACE**  
> **PRIMARY TALENT: SHADOW ECHO**
>
---

The words pulsed like they were alive.

John stared at them.

"A game system?" he muttered.

He had played enough games to recognize one.

Except this wasn't a monitor.

This wasn't a dream.

This felt real.

Very real.

---
>
> **WARNING**  
> **MEMORIES AND PERSONALITY DETERMINE STARTING SKILLS**  
> **SUBJECT MINDSET: SURVIVAL / POWER ACQUISITION**
>
---

More text appeared.

---
>
>**ABILITY UNLOCKED**  
>**SHADOW SPACE**
>
---

A personal extradimensional storage realm.

Non-living objects may be stored.

Objects remain perfectly preserved.

Access requires mental focus.

---

Another line appeared.

---
>
>**PRIMARY TALENT**  
>**SHADOW ECHO**
>
>You may create one permanent Shadow Clone per level gained.
>
>Clones train and learn within Shadow Space.
>
>All knowledge transfers to the originator.
>
>Each clone amplifies your base statistics.
>
>Clones can not interact with the real world.
---

John stared at the glowing words.

Slowly, something unfamiliar crept into his chest.

Hope.

Power.

Maybe for the first time in his life… he wouldn’t be helpless.

Another message appeared.

---
>
>**WORLD INITIALIZATION COMPLETE**  
>**REINCARNATION BEGINNING**  
>**WELCOME TO YOUR SECOND LIFE**
>
---

The void shattered.

Light exploded around him.

And John was reborn.

## Waking up

Pain dragged John back into existence.

It started in his chest.

Then his head.

Then everywhere else.

He sucked in a ragged breath and immediately choked on the taste of blood and dirt.

“Cough—!”

His body convulsed and sharp agony shot through his ribs.

For a long moment he simply lay there, gasping like a fish thrown onto land.

Above him stretched a wide blue sky framed by jagged gray stone.

Wind whispered over the cliff face.

Birds circled high above.

John blinked slowly.

*Sky…?*

That didn’t make sense.

The last thing he remembered was crossing a street.

The roar of a horn.

A massive truck.

Impact.

Then darkness.

Then…

The System.

Reincarnation.

John forced his head to turn.

Loose rocks crunched under his cheek.

He was lying at the bottom of a steep cliff in what looked like a narrow ravine. Tall grass and scattered shrubs clung stubbornly to cracks in the stone walls.

Blood soaked the rough tunic covering his chest.

*This body…* That thought triggered something.

Memories surged forward.

Not his memories.

Someone else’s.

A young man.

Thin.

Poor.

Living in a small wooden house on the outskirts of a medieval town.

Working long days hauling grain and chopping wood for copper coins.

And being mocked for it.

Four boys.

Well-fed.

Well-dressed.

The sons of merchants and minor nobles.

They had cornered him on the cliff road outside town.

Laughing.

Shoving.

Punching.

Calling him a rat.

A beggar.

A useless nobody.

The young man had tried to run.

They beat him for that.

Then one of them shoved him toward the edge.

The others laughed.

Someone said something like— *"Let’s see if he can fly."*

A push.

A stumble.

A scream.

Then falling.

John closed his eyes.

“So that’s how you died.”

The boy in this body had been just like him.

Weak.

Alone.

Easy prey.

John slowly clenched a fist in the dirt.

“Bullies…”

Even across two lives the pattern hadn’t changed.

Different world.

Same kind of people.

People born with money and power who thought that meant they could do anything.

Including murder.

The anger felt familiar.

Old.

But this time something else was there too.

Power.

A faint blue glow appeared in front of his eyes.

---
>
>**REINCARNATION COMPLETE**  
>**HOST BODY STABILIZING**  
>**CRITICAL INJURIES DETECTED**
>
---

John let out a slow breath.

“Good… the system’s still here.”

More words appeared.

---
>
>**CLASS: WEAPON SUMMONER**  
>**CORE ABILITY: SHADOW SPACE**  
>**PRIMARY TALENT: SHADOW ECHO**  
>**LEVEL: 1**
>
---

Another notification blinked.

---
>
>**SHADOW CLONE AVAILABLE**  
>
---

John laughed weakly, then immediately regretted it as pain stabbed through his ribs.

Even so, a grin spread across his bloodstained face.

One clone.

At level one.

That meant his stats were already doubled.

Not bad for someone who had just died.

He slowly pushed himself into a sitting position using a nearby rock.

The body he inhabited was young.

Lean but underfed.

Calloused hands from labor.

Maybe seventeen or eighteen years old.

Not strong.

But alive.

Above him the cliff rose sharply, easily thirty meters high. Loose soil and broken stones marked the path where this body had tumbled down.

John stared at it.

Somewhere up there were four boys who thought they had just gotten away with murder.

The same type of people who had always gotten away with everything.

He wiped blood from his mouth.

“This time…”

His voice came out rough.

“I’m not taking it.”

The system flickered again.

---
>
>**ABILITY AVAILABLE**  
>Create Shadow Clone?  
>**YES / NO**
>
---

John didn’t hesitate.

“Yes.”

Something inside his mind shifted.

It felt strange.

Like a piece of his consciousness stepped sideways.  His energy dropped like a stone, his mana syphoned out of him, all of it.

Suddenly he could sense it.

A vast dark space.

Endless.

Silent.

And standing inside it…

Was him.

A perfect copy.

His Shadow Clone.

Another message appeared.

---
>
>**SHADOW SPACE INITIALIZED**  
>**1 SHADOW CLONE ACTIVE**  
>
---

John leaned back against the rock and exhaled.

The clone inside Shadow Space stood waiting.

Ready to train.

Ready to learn.

Ready to grow stronger.

And everything it learned would become his.

A slow smile spread across John’s face.

“If I survive long enough…”

His eyes hardened.

“…those bastards are going to regret today.”

His last thought before he passed out.
