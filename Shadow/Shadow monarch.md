# Shadow Monarch

### Awakening Day

The room around him buzzed, the last eighteen years of school and training have all been in preparation of this day.  Every student was talking, dreaming, hoping to awaken a talent.  They know their is only about a five percent chance to awaken a talent, but everyone thinks they are special.

People with talents gain a special status, laws generally don't apply, money comes easy, status is built in.  There is a down side to it to, they are expected to fight the beasts outside the cities.  They must continually train, get stronger to fight back the beasts allowing the humans to live in relative peace. 

Mike sat in his little corner, nobody seeking him out for conversation.  Mike was effectively invisible, and that is how Mike liked it.  He is the seventh son of the city lord there are no special considerations for him, no high expectations, by all rights he could be a layabout and nobody would think poorly of him.   As it stands Mike was average, average scores on the cultural exams, average strength tests.  His sparring record shows him not strong enough to be remembered or week enough to be pitied, just an average person.

To be honest Mike was all ready tired hearing about the awakening ceremony, he just wanted to get it over with.  With a mere five percent chance of awakening odds where firmly against him becoming a warrior, he was looking forward to the chance to be a lay about, live off his fathers legacy, be the stereotypical second generation rich kid.

The instructor's voice finally cut through the noise. "Line up, alphabetical order.  I know we have gone over this a thousand times but here it is once again.  We will walk in line to the auditorium, one person at a time will approach the awakening stone.  You will then place your hand on the stone, if you awaken the stone will react and you will gain a mark on your hand showing you have an awakened talent.  No reaction, no talent, and you will move along making room for the next person."

Mike barley listened, for the last month this has been the primary topic.  For most of the people in this room this was their one and only chance to rise beyond the ordinary.  Mike wanted nothing more than to be ordinary.

A quick walk and about ten minutes later they were all in line at the auditorium.  A plane looking stone sat on a table on the stage, the last class has just finished, some students with ear to ear smiles, the vast majority with looks of envy on their faces.  

Class three, Mikes class began to go through the procedure.  Occasionally there would be a flicker or a glow from the stone and the person on the stage would smile and show off their mark. Most moved to the other side disappointed.  When Mike was up he thought to himself, "Finally, lets get this over with."

Mike placed his hand on the stone, for a moment, nothing, Mike fully relaxed.  "Time to be a lay about, no more daily training I can finally relax."  Then the stone reacted, not a small flicker, not a bright glow, the stone turned dark, blacker than any black Mike had ever seen before, it seemed to absorb the light in the room.  Then the burning began, it started on the back of Mikes right hand, spread down his arm, across his back and up his left arm.

As quick as it begun it was over, the room returned to normal.  The awakening proctor simply stated, "awakening complete, next."  Mike moved to the edge of the stage where the class waited, he looked at the mark on the back of his right hand dense, dark lines eerily complex, the mark would be photographed and uploaded to a database later, it would be his identification from now on.  All Mike could think is "Well, so much for the easy life."  With a sigh he prepared himself for the hours of paperwork coming up.
