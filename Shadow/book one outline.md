Here is a **detailed Book One outline** structured like a typical **LitRPG progression novel (30–35 chapters)**. It focuses on **survival, early dungeon grinding, system discovery, and the first revenge arc**.

Book 1 ends at **Level 10** with the revolver unlock.

---

# Book 1 – Ashes of the Weak

Shadow Summoner Series

Core themes:

* survival
* rage turned into discipline
* power earned through grinding
* the first step toward revenge

---

# ACT 1 — Rebirth (Chapters 1–5)

Goal: establish the world, system, and John's motivation.

## Chapter 1 – The Bottom of the Cliff

* John awakens in a dead young man’s body.
* Memories reveal the boy was beaten and thrown from a cliff.
* The bullies are sons of wealthy townsfolk.
* System appears.
* John creates his **first Shadow Clone** and passes out.

---

## Chapter 2 – Walking Wounded

* John wakes at sunset with zero mana.
* He summons the **rapier**.
* Begins walking toward the village.
* Fights several low-level predators (wolves, fox-beasts).
* Gains first experience.

---

## Chapter 3 – Ashes

* John reaches home.
* The house is burned down.
* Finds the mother’s corpse.
* Realizes the bullies covered their tracks.
* Understands the village will protect them.
* Decides revenge must wait.

---

## Chapter 4 – Into the Forest

* John heads into the forest.
* Learns how **Shadow Space works**.
* Observes his clone training.
* Hunts small monsters to survive.

---

## Chapter 5 – The Dungeon Entrance

* John finds the **Forest Dungeon**.
* Adventurers rarely enter because it's low value.
* Perfect grinding location.
* He enters.

---

# ACT 2 — Grinding for Power (Chapters 6–18)

Goal: level progression and mastery of abilities.

---

## Chapter 6 – Dire Rats

* First dungeon enemies.
* Learns real combat pacing.
* Rapier fighting improves.

---

## Chapter 7 – System Growth

* Levels up to **Level 2**.
* Gains **second clone**.
* Stats increase dramatically.

---

## Chapter 8 – The Flintlock

* Experiments with the **flintlock pistol**.
* Learns reload times are slow.
* Clones begin practicing firearm drills.

---

## Chapter 9 – Goblin Ambush

* First intelligent enemies.
* Goblins attempt coordinated attack.
* John learns positioning.

---

## Chapter 10 – Dungeon Ecology

* John maps the first dungeon floor.
* Learns monster spawn patterns.

---

## Chapter 11 – The Hunter

* John becomes efficient.
* Begins hunting monsters instead of reacting.

---

## Chapter 12 – Level 4

* Now has **4 clones**.
* Clones train constantly:

  * sword
  * firearms
  * tactics
  * stamina drills

---

## Chapter 13 – Cave Wolves

* Pack predators.
* First serious danger.

---

## Chapter 14 – Rifle Training

* John uses the **muzzle-loader rifle**.
* High damage but slow reload.

---

## Chapter 15 – The Hobgoblin

* Mini-boss fight.
* Brutal close combat.

---

## Chapter 16 – Level 6

* Stats begin snowballing due to clone amplification.
* John realizes how powerful his ability really is.

---

## Chapter 17 – First Real Loot

* Finds:

  * potions
  * armor
  * coins

---

## Chapter 18 – The First Floor Boss

* Large monster (Dire Boar or Alpha Wolf).
* Hard battle.
* John wins narrowly.

He leaves the dungeon stronger.

---

# ACT 3 — The Return (Chapters 19–28)

Goal: revenge setup and confrontation.

---

## Chapter 19 – Rumors

* John returns near the village.
* Hears rumors about:

  * the burned house
  * the “dead boy”

---

## Chapter 20 – Watching the Bullies

* John observes them secretly.
* They brag about the murder.

---

## Chapter 21 – The First Kill

* John ambushes one bully alone.
* Silent rapier kill.

---

## Chapter 22 – Panic in Town

* Body discovered.
* Guards begin searching.

---

## Chapter 23 – The Second and Third

* John eliminates two more in separate encounters.

---

## Chapter 24 – The Last Bully

* Final bully hides at family estate.

---

## Chapter 25 – Mansion Infiltration

* John sneaks inside the estate at night.

---

## Chapter 26 – The Fight

* Last bully attacks with guards.

---

## Chapter 27 – Escape

* John kills the bully but must flee.

---

## Chapter 28 – Fire

* Before leaving town, John burns the mansion.

The symbol mirrors what they did to his home.

---

# ACT 4 — Leaving the Past (Chapters 29–32)

Goal: transition into larger world.

---

## Chapter 29 – Manhunt

* Guards search for the killer.
* John's identity almost exposed.

---

## Chapter 30 – Level 10

After final dungeon grinding.

System message appears:

**Level 10 reached**  

New weapons unlocked:

* Colt Peacemaker revolver
* Lever-action rifle

---

## Chapter 31 – A Bigger World

John learns about:

* Adventurer guilds
* larger dungeons
* cities
* noble politics

---

## Chapter 32 – Into the Wild

John leaves the region.

His clones now train constantly.

He plans something bigger.

Revenge on the system that lets monsters rule society.

Final line:

*"This world runs on power."*  

*"So I'll take all of it."*
