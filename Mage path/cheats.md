# James History
## First Life (2345–2545)

Born: 2345

Born in an era where the Ring System defined magical hierarchy.

Displayed rare mana affinity and analytical genius from childhood.

Dedicated his life to magical research, rune engineering, and combat refinement.

## 2445 — Eighth Ring Achievement

After a century of relentless study and battlefield experience, James successfully formed his Eighth Ring.

Entered the ranks of the world’s strongest Grandmasters.

Began long-term preparation for the mythical Ninth Ring.

## 2445–2545 — The Century of Preparation

Refined mana compression methods.

Experimented with soul reinforcement techniques.

Studied ancient ruins magical fragments.

Observed the world deteriorate as dark magic destoyed eco systems and other mages.

Realized the Ninth Ring was not just power — it was transformation.

## 2545 — The Catastrophe

Attempted to form the Ninth Ring.  forced into the situation not ready but desperate times, dark magic creatures and former mages are attacking and he needs the addtional power to fight them off.

Mana density exceeded physical tollerance

The breakthrough triggered a dimensional fracture.

James body was destroyed when the ring formed

## Regression — Year 1345

Reborn exactly 1,000 years earlier.

Born as the seventh son of a noble house.

Retains:

Full memory

Ring structures (spiritually intact)

Magical theory and combat experience

Limitations:

Child’s body

Fragile mana channels

Muscles and nerves unable to handle high-density mana flow

Critical Twist

The nine rings are with him, the ability to shape the world is a part of him but his body cannot handle the mana flow for large spells.  

As it sits right now he can only cast first ring spells, still enough to shock the world around him.

As he circulates the mana within him his body will be toughend and the mana channels will open and streangthen.
