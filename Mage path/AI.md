# Prologue

## Year 2545 — The Ninth Ring

The sky was wrong.

It had been wrong for decades — fractured by mana storms, scarred by reactor flares, stained with the auroras of broken ley lines — but tonight it was *splitting*.

Above the shattered spires of the Western Continent’s last Mage Tower, the heavens folded inward like torn silk.

At the center of the storm stood James.

Eight Rings rotated behind him.

They were not physical halos, but vast geometries of compressed mana — luminous circles layered in impossible symmetry. Each Ring hummed at a different frequency, harmonizing into a chord that bent the air and silenced the battlefield below.

Around him lay ruin.

Archmages burned in spirals of failed ascension.
Artificial mana reactors screamed as their cores destabilized.
Creatures born from corrupted rituals clawed their way through collapsing dimensions.

The Ring Wars had already devoured the oceans.

Tonight, they would devour the sky.

James exhaled slowly.

He had prepared for a century.

Mana compression matrices refined to theoretical perfection.
Soul-lattice reinforcement etched into his very being.
Dimensional tolerance calculations run ten million times in meditation.

But he had not meant to ascend tonight.

He had wanted another decade.

Another year.

Another hour.

A tide of dark entities surged toward the Tower’s foundation — former mages twisted by overexposure, their Rings shattered and fused into grotesque spirals of unstable mana. Behind them, a fracture in reality widened, bleeding void-light into the world.

If the breach expanded, the continent would fold into nothing.

There was no time left.

James raised his hand.

The Eight Rings shifted.

Compressed.

Aligned.

Mana density climbed past the threshold any human body was meant to endure.

His bones began to sing.

Not metaphorically — they vibrated as mana forced its way through channels reinforced a thousand times over. His veins glowed silver-blue. The air crystallized around him.

He reached inward.

The Ninth Ring was not something constructed.

It was something *unlocked*.

A boundary between mortal containment and something beyond structure.

He compressed further.

Skin split along lines of light.

Blood evaporated before it could fall.

Below, the corrupted archmages screamed as gravity reversed and mana pressure crushed them into spirals of ash.

The dimensional fracture widened.

James forced his consciousness deeper — past instinct, past pain, past the memory of flesh.

He saw it then.

The pattern behind the Rings.

Not circles — but a spiral.

Not power — but transformation.

The Ninth Ring ignited.

Reality went silent.

For a single heartbeat, the storm froze.

Then everything shattered.

Mana density exceeded biological tolerance.

Soul and body desynchronized.

The Eight Rings collapsed inward, merging into a single blinding axis.

James felt his physical form unravel — nerves dissolving into light, muscles disintegrating into motes of compressed energy.

There was no fear.

Only clarity.

The world had gone too far.

Artificial reactors. Ring militarization. Ascension as arms race.

He had participated.

He had refined the system that destroyed the sky.

The Ninth Ring was not transcendence.

It was correction.

As the dimensional fracture consumed the Tower, James did not resist.

He redirected.

He twisted the spiral.

He anchored his soul to a coordinate far below the fracture’s origin — a temporal depth calculated a century ago during theoretical experiments he had hoped never to use.

If the Age of Ascension was inevitable—

—then it must be begun differently.

Light swallowed him.

The storm imploded.

The Tower vanished.

Across the continent, mana pressure collapsed to silence.

And in the void between seconds, a soul wrapped in nine sealed Rings fell backward through time.

---

## Year 1345

A child gasped.

Thin lungs dragged in their first breath.

In a quiet noble estate far from any Mage Tower — in an era where the Fifth Ring was myth and the system incomplete — the seventh son opened his eyes.

Memory returned instantly.

The Ring Wars.
The fracture.
The Ninth ignition.

Deep within him, beyond fragile flesh and newborn nerves, nine perfect structures rotated in silent harmony.

The body was small.

The channels narrow.

The world unprepared.

James did not cry.

He simply stared at the wooden ceiling beams above his bed and began calculating.

This time—

The sky would not break.
