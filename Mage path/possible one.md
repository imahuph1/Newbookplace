# Chapter one

## Year 2545 - The end of the world

The sky was wrong.  It had been worstening for decades. Fractured by dark mana, scarred by battles between mages and the dark creatures of dark mana.  Tonight it was *splitting*.

Above the shattered spires of James Mage tower, the hevens folded inward like torn silk.  At the center of the storm stood James.  Eight rings roted within him.  They were not physical halos, but vast mana stores. Each ring hummed within him at a differnet frequency,, harmonizing into a chord that bend the air and silenced the battlefield below.  Around him lay ruin.

Archmages burned in spirals of failed ascension. Creatures born from corrupted rituals clawed their way through collapsing dimesions.  The war with the beasts had allready devoured the oceans.  Tonight, they would devour the sky.  James exhaled slowly, he had prepared for a century.  He had not meant to attempt to reach the ninth ring tonight, he was not ready, he had wanted another week to prepair, another another decade, another century.  

A tide of dark entities surged toward the Tower's foundation. Former mages twised by overexposure to dark mana, their rings shattered and fused into grotesque spirals.  Behind them, a fracture in reality widened, bleeding void-light into the world.

If the breach expanded, the continent would fall to the dark mana creatures.   There was no time left.  If he does not reach the ninth ring tonight he would die, this continent would die.  James raised his hand.  The rings within him shifted, alighned.  The mana within him climed past the threshold any human body was ment to endure.  His bones began to sing.  

They vibraed as mana forced its way through channels reinfoced thousands of times over.  His veins glowed silver-blue.  The air seemed to crystalize around him.  He reached inward.  The ninth ring was not something constructed, it was something *unlocked*.  A boundry between mortal containment and something beyond structure.

As he drew in more mana, purified it, and forced it along allready overburdened channels his skin split, blood evaporated before it could fall.  Below the corrupted archmages screamed as the mana pressure crushed them into spirals of ash.  James forced his consciousness deeper, past instinct, past pain, past the memory of flesh.

The ninth ring ignited, James felt it, not a mere power increase but a transformation.  James had stepped beyond mortality.  Reality went silent, for a single heartbeat, the storm froze.  Then everything shattered.  The nine rings collapsed inward, James felt his physical form unravel, nerves dissolving into light, muscles disintergrating into motes of compressed energy.   There was no fear, only clarity.  He had gone to far, to fast, the energy was to much for him to handle, to control.   As the dimesional fracture consumed the Tower, James was unable to resist.  

The dark mana swallowed what was left of his body, the Tower vanshed.  Across the continent, mana pressure collapsed to silence.   In the moment between seconds, a soul wrapped in nine rings fell backward through time.
