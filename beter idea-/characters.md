# Children of Destiny 
## **1. Leon Windfall — The Sword That Learned to Stay**

**Public Persona:** Cold, taciturn sword genius\
**Private Reality:** Socially feral

Leon speaks in short sentences because full ones feel like emotional overextension.

**Running Gag:**

* Says things like:\
  “Formation… acceptable.”\
  Which everyone eventually learns means _high praise_.

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike constantly says:

> “Stick with us, Leon. Going solo really cuts into teamwork.”

Leon does not laugh.\
He _does_ reposition to cover the flank.

**Real Consequences:**

* Early on, Leon loses a teammate in a mock battlefield exercise because he overextends.

* He never forgets it.

* Becomes obsessively protective afterward.

**Mike’s Pressure Method:**\
Mike intentionally puts Leon in command when he’s exhausted.

> “You’re tired. Good. So are your men. Lead anyway.”

**Resulting Change:**\
Leon becomes terrifying—not because his sword is sharp, but because **his unit never breaks**.

***

## **2. Iris Frostveil — The Calm That Learned to Burn**

**Public Persona:** Untouchable ice beauty\
**Private Reality:** Terrified of losing control

She suppresses emotion so hard that her qi _literally crystallizes too cleanly_.

**Running Gag:**\
Mike constantly ruins her dramatic moments.

Iris (coldly):

> “Victory was inevitable.”

Mike:

> “So was winter. Still unpleasant.”

She almost freezes him once. Accidentally. On purpose.

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike refers to her techniques as:

> “Emotionally unavailable martial arts.”

**Real Consequences:**

* When Mike forces her into a desperate battle, her composure cracks.

* She loses partial control.

* Frostbite scars her own meridians permanently.

She keeps the scars.

**Mike’s Pressure Method:**\
Mike _provokes_ her emotionally mid-fight.

> “If this is all you’ve got, the Phoenix is just a decorative bird.”

**Resulting Change:**\
Her Ice Phoenix awakens **violent**, not pristine.

She laughs once during battle.

Everyone else is alarmed.

***

## **3. Bran Ironhart — The Wall That Learned Angles**

**Public Persona:** Loud, unkillable brute\
**Private Reality:** Afraid he’s only useful as a shield

**Running Gag:**\
Bran counts tactics aloud.

> “Okay. Plan A: punch.\
> Plan B: punch harder.\
> Plan C: ask Evan.”

This becomes… actual strategy.

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike says:

> “Brains _and_ brawn, Bran. You’re becoming a well-rounded individual.”

Bran replies:

> “I am very round.”

**Real Consequences:**

* Bran overrelies on regeneration once.

* It fails.

* He fights three months with cracked bones because _he refuses to stop_.

Mike lets him.

**Mike’s Pressure Method:**\
Mike beats him without hitting him.

Just footwork. Terrain. Timing.

> “You didn’t lose because you’re weak.\
> You lost because you were predictable.”

**Resulting Change:**\
Bran starts thinking _before_ charging.

Which somehow makes him scarier.

***

## **4. Mira Greenflame — The One Everyone Tries to Keep Safe**

**Public Persona:** Gentle support\
**Private Reality:** Tactical monster in denial

**Running Gag:**\
Mira apologizes while ruining lives.

> “Sorry—this pill may cause mild combustion.”

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike calls her:

> “The most dangerous person with a mortar and pestle.”

**Real Consequences:**

* Forced into frontline alchemy once.

* A mistake poisons an ally—non-lethally, but badly.

* She does not sleep for a week afterward.

**Mike’s Pressure Method:**\
Mike hands her incomplete ingredients mid-fight.

> “You’ve got ten seconds. Or Bran bleeds.”

She delivers in eight.

**Resulting Change:**\
Enemies learn to target her first.

She learns to enjoy out-preparing them.

***

## **5. Drake Stormward — The Arrogance That Learned Patience**

**Public Persona:** Lightning prodigy\
**Private Reality:** Deeply afraid of becoming irrelevant

**Running Gag:**\
Drake announces attacks like a narrator.

> “Witness—”

Mike interrupts:

> “No one asked.”

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike says:

> “You’re very shocking, Drake.”

Drake hates that it’s accurate.

**Real Consequences:**

* Public humiliation by Mike fractures Drake’s pride.

* He almost quits cultivation entirely.

* Only stays because Mike trains him _in private_.

**Mike’s Pressure Method:**\
Mike makes Drake protect weaker allies.

> “If they die, your speed was useless.”

**Resulting Change:**\
Drake becomes terrifyingly precise.

Lightning that _waits_.

***

## **6. Evan Runewright — The Brain Forced to Move**

**Public Persona:** Frail genius\
**Private Reality:** Paralyzed by possibility

**Running Gag:**\
Evan starts explaining a plan.

Mike:

> “You have five seconds or we’re improvising.”

They improvise.

It usually works.

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike says:

> “Overthinking is just thinking with commitment issues.”

**Real Consequences:**

* Evan freezes once.

* An array deploys late.

* Someone is seriously injured.

He never freezes again.

**Mike’s Pressure Method:**\
Mike _breaks Evan’s formations on purpose_.

> “Reality doesn’t respect perfect diagrams.”

**Resulting Change:**\
Evan learns to design formations that **expect failure**.

They’re better.

***

## **7. Shade Blackthorn — The Knife That Learned Names**

**Public Persona:** Silent assassin\
**Private Reality:** Has no idea how friendships work

**Running Gag:**\
Shade stands too close.

Mike:

> “Personal space exists.”

Shade:

> “Acknowledged.”\
> (Does not move.)

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike calls her:

> “Our emotional support assassin.”

She does not know what that means.\
She likes it.

**Real Consequences:**

* Shade hesitates once—to protect instead of kill.

* She gets wounded.

* The order that raised her marks her as defective.

Mike does not let them reclaim her.

**Mike’s Pressure Method:**\
Mike assigns her non-lethal missions.

> “Today, you guard. No killing.”

She struggles more with this than assassination.

**Resulting Change:**\
Shade learns loyalty.

Which makes her far deadlier than before.

# **8. Kael Sunspire — The Hero Who Learned to Share the Burden**

**Public Persona:** Radiant righteous prodigy\
**Private Reality:** Suffocating under expectation

Kael was born for greatness.

Everyone told him so.\
Sect elders. Instructors. Civilians he saved by accident.

He believes them.

That’s the problem.

**Running Gag:**\
Kael insists on self-sacrificial plays.

Mike sighs every time.

> “Kael. If you die dramatically again, I’m billing you for emotional damages.”

Kael responds:

> “Someone has to bear the light.”

Mike:

> “Yeah. _Plural_. That’s how shoulders work.”

**Dad-Joke Victim Status:**\
Mike calls his cultivation:

> “Weaponized martyrdom.”

Kael does not deny this.

***

### **Real Consequences**

* Kael overextends in a major academy conflict.

* He wins.

* Three allies are critically wounded covering the gap he left behind.

Kael calls it necessary.

No one else does.

He starts training alone at night.

***

### **Mike’s Pressure Method**

Mike **refuses** to let Kael take the lead in a critical mission.

Instead, he assigns Kael as _support_.

> “You don’t get to be the sun today.\
> You get to be warmth.”

Kael hates it.

He almost disobeys.

***

### **Resulting Change**

Kael learns that leadership is not about standing in front.

It’s about **trusting others not to fall without you**.

His light changes.

Less blinding.\
Far more lethal.

***

# **9. Nyx Voidweaver — The Lone Survivor Who Learned to Stay**

**Public Persona:** Detached void cultivator\
**Private Reality:** Terrified of attachment

Nyx believes in one thing:

> _Everything dies. Attachment just decides how much it hurts._

She does not plan for victory.\
She plans for aftermath.

**Running Gag:**\
Nyx disappears without warning.

Mike always notices.

Later:

> “You know, normal people say goodbye.”

Nyx:

> “That increases probability of regret.”

Mike:

> “So does abandonment.”

She stops leaving without warning.

Eventually.

***

### **Dad-Joke Victim Status**

Mike calls her cultivation:

> “Emotionally minimalist.”

Nyx responds:

> “Excess causes instability.”

Mike:

> “So does isolation. Ask your void.”

She does not respond.

But she listens.

***

### **Real Consequences**

* Nyx abandons a losing battlefield early.

* It is the _correct_ tactical choice.

* Two teammates barely survive without her suppression.

She tells herself it was optimal.

She trains harder alone.

***

### **Mike’s Pressure Method**

Mike pairs her with Shade.

Non-negotiable.

> “If you leave, she dies.”

Nyx almost breaks formation.

She doesn’t.

***

### **Resulting Change**

Nyx learns something horrifying:

Staying is scarier than leaving.

But it makes her void **anchored**.

And anchored voids do not collapse.

# **Why the Nine Die in the Vision (Original Timeline)**

Each of them fails _alone_:

* Leon overprotects and isolates himself.

* Iris suppresses until she shatters.

* Bran tanks damage no one should endure.

* Mira hesitates when lives are on the line.

* Drake overcommits to prove relevance.

* Evan freezes at a branching possibility.

* Shade chooses one life over many.

* Kael sacrifices himself unnecessarily.

* Nyx retreats and survives alone.

The enemy doesn’t overpower them.

It **waits**.

***

# **Why Mike Is Dangerous**

Mike is not only the strongest.

He is the **only one who saw the ending**.

He doesn’t teach them how to win.

He teaches them how to **fail together and still stand**.

That is why the System stopped giving quests.

That is why it only showed him the future.

> Because if even one of them learns to lean—\
> the vision breaks.

***


# **Group Dynamic (With Tone)**

* They argue constantly.

* They trust absolutely.

* They joke _after_ battle, not during.

* Mike is never the leader.

* Mike rarely acts in battles beyond his role.

* If Mike is forced to act, they fear training the next day.

* Everyone knows he’s the bare minimum, they want to be better than that.

**Unofficial Motto:**

> “If Mike bled, we messed up.”

**Official Academy Opinion:**

> “That rich second-generation kid is carried by monsters.”

The monsters disagree.
