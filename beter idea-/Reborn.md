# Mike reborn

## **Part 1: Early Years (Birth – Age 10)**

**Age 0–1:**

* Mike is reborn in a wealthy, powerful family.

* Mechanical voice in his head speaks nonsense; slowly teaches him the local language.

* By **5 months**, he understands spoken/written language.

* By **1 year**, he converses fluently, walks, and interacts with adults.

**Age 1–5:**

* Daily tasks from the system: stretches, focus exercises, observation.

* Weekly tasks: small cultivation and martial arts techniques.

* Gains incremental boosts to **vitality, mental power, vision**.

* Comedic moments: infant Mike appears precocious, slightly unsettling adults with intelligence beyond his age.

**Age 6–8:**

* **Age 6:** Starts secret cultivation, unknown to parents.

* **Age 8:** Begins martial arts techniques.

* Appears as a gifted child but never draws attention to his true abilities.

* System guides him quietly, with daily/weekly exercises, gradually increasing his strength.

***

## **Part 2: Awakening & Vision (~Age 10–12)**

**Awakening Day:**

* Receives **Primordial Body** and **10,000x cultivation speed**.

* Gains the ability to refine **body, qi, and mind** faster than anyone else.

* System upgrades: no more tasks, instead delivers a **warning vision**.

**The Vision:**

* Shows **nine prodigies** at their peak power.

* They fight a future enemy who exploits their individual weaknesses, killing them one by one.

* Enemy eventually devastates the planet.

* Mike realizes that to prevent this, he must intervene **early**, before the prodigies reach full maturity.

**Decision:**

* Befriend them early.

* Train them as a **team**, not just individually.

* Push them to their limits while hiding his true strength.

* Comedic irony: Mike now has a **massive secret** while everyone sees him as a rich, slightly talented kid.

***

## **Part 3: School Years & Formation of the Children of Destiny (~Age 12–18)**

**Age 12–14:**

* Joins the best academy, appearing as a wealthy second-generation student.

* AI Jeeves helps Identify the nine prodigies

* All prodigies get into the best academy on their own merits.

* Subtly analyzes their weaknesses (from vision) and starts guiding them.

**Age 14–16:**

* Begins **private training sessions** with select students.

* Pushes the group to grow individually **and as a team**, with Mike strategically orchestrating exercises and sparring.

* Appears just slightly stronger than top prodigies, hiding his true cultivation.

* Comedic moments: Mike plays the “naive rich kid” while secretly mentoring everyone.

**Age 16–18:**

* Team officially forms: **Children of Destiny**.

* Mike emphasizes teamwork over individual glory.

* Uses competitions and missions to develop **cohesion, strategy, and complementary skills**.

* Laughs and pranks maintain social charm, hiding his ruthlessness in battle/training.

***

## **Part 4: College & Pre-Crisis (~Age 18–22)**

* Each member is now extraordinarily powerful, capable of changing regions/worlds.

* Mike remains the anchor, orchestrating growth quietly.

* Team starts facing high-level threats together, learning to **fight as one**.

* Comedic elements: outsiders misjudge Mike as “rich kid relying on parents,” not knowing he’s the **strongest in secret**.

* Small arcs: each prodigy confronts personal weaknesses, often through Mike’s strategic guidance.

***

## **Part 5: Post-College & Crisis (~Age 22–25)**

* Prodigies take separate paths, continuing individual growth.

* occasional small scale team ups, to clear secret realms, beast tides.

* Mike maintains contact, serving as the central node of guidance.

* all reach the peak of cultivation (immortal realm)

* The **enemy from the vision appears**, exploiting weaknesses that Mike had foreseen.

* Team reunites, fighting together.

* Mike finally **stops holding back**, revealing the full extent of his power and cultivation.

**Climactic Battle:**

* Enemy attempts to isolate and kill each prodigy.

* Mike orchestrates team tactics and joins the fight personally.

* Prodigies realize **Mike’s hidden strength** and how he had been shaping them from childhood.

* Team synergy and Mike’s guidance overcome the enemy, saving the planet.

***

## **Part 6: Aftermath & Legacy**

* Children of Destiny go their separate ways again, but maintain bonds.

* Mike continues as the **quiet anchor**, ensuring the group remains prepared for future threats.

* Humor and camaraderie continue, juxtaposed with his overwhelming power.

* Potential setup for sequels: new visions, new prodigies, new threats.
