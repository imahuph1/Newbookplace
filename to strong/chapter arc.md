# The Wrath of a Quiet Man

## Rebirth

Reborn in the body of a young man.  The body has been beaten to the point of death.  Malnourished, clothes that are closer to rags than cloths.  Left in a heap in an alley.  John Smith the name is the same but the memories of the previous host show a sad living condition.  School during the day, working a poor paying job at an internet cafe after school, paying to stay their overnight.  Enough left over for just enough food to live on and a membership at a twenty four hour gym, a place he can shower and wash his cloths.  

When time comes to graduate from school it is also awakening day.  Everyone has a chance to get a boon, a gift from the world.  Some get combat related gifts, others get crafting or farming related gifts.  A few even get merchant or sales related gifts.  Then their is John, he gets the lowest possible rank, F, and his gift serves no useful purpose, Big cock and brass balls.  

Once again humiliated by this world John simply continues his life, the only change is a day job, scrubbing the floors of a butcher shop, and his evening job, running the desk of the internet cafe.  Now he makes enough to eat slightly better and have slightly better clothes.
