# The Wrath of a Quiet Man

## Rebirth

Reborn in the body of a young man.  The body has been beaten to the point of death.  Malnourished, clothes that are closer to rags than cloths.  Left in a heap in an alley.  John Smith the name is the same but the memories of the previous host show a sad living condition.  A world with cultivators and beasts.  School during the day, working a poor paying job at an internet cafe after school, paying to stay their overnight. Enough left over for just enough food to live on and a membership at a twenty four hour gym, a place he can shower and wash his cloths.  

When time comes to graduate from school it is also awakening day.  Everyone awakens their spiritual root.  Most get average spiritual roots, a few lucky people get high tier spiritual roots,  John awakes the worst possible spiritual roots.

Once again humiliated by this world John simply continues his life, the only change is a day job, scrubbing the floors of a butcher shop, and his evening job, running the desk of the internet cafe.  Now he makes enough to eat slightly better and have slightly better clothes.

## The voice

do you want to be rich, to be powerful, simply say yes
with me you can have wealth and power
all you have to do is not actively fight
engage in a fight and the stat growth will stop
the doubling of all stats last for a year
if you last the full year, you will be the strongest being in the universe.

why, this gift is offered to stagnate worlds, a little bit of chaos can go a long way

controlling that kind of power wisdom and intelligence allow the fine control of strength 

the temptation, after a month you will be stronger than most
two months strongest being on the planet
you could stop the doubling their and take revenge on all those who have wronged you.

what does the voice get out of it, when he dies the power is returned to him with any growth john has accumulated. 

## A year of temptation

over the course of a year, he is mugged, sees women being beaten and kidnaped, beasts invade the city, each of these he could have stopped, but he simply keeps his routines.
the voice keeps attempting to have him intervene, to fight, to stop the unnecessary deaths, the violence.  John ignores the voice, he keeps his routine, take the occasional beating as if this is a normal life.  For him it is.  He does not believe he has the power to stop anything, he does not believe that if he fights he will have the power the voice says he will.  

At the end of the year, when all the stats hit him at once, his biggest problem is that now he lives in a world to him seams like tissue paper.

## Ultimate power, Ultimate apathy

Years of being nobody, of being nothing, have lead not only to severe psychological trauma, huge self esteem issues, but of apathy.  John really no longer cares.

John now has the power to change the world, heck he could probably put a stop to the entire universe if he wanted to.  he could have unlimited riches, he could finish the invasions coming to his planet.  His mere presence could stop future wars.  But why, no matter the society the greed and corruption remains.

## Into the wilds

Johns rapid cultivation created a large disturbance, not wanting to be bothered John took a step forward and traveled several miles into the wilds around the city.

He needed time to think, time to decide how he was going to use this new found power.  According to the voice he is now the most powerful being in the universe.  He had no idea how to test this.

Relaxing by a fire, a recently killed beast roasting over it.  A girl a year or two older than him approaches the fire and politely asks him if she could rest here.

She belongs to a mid level family and is currently on the run from another mid ranked family.  She found a rare herb and has been targeted.  She is trying to get home before they catch up, but is tired and needs to rest, she is pretty sure she lost them.  

She (Heather) does not know that the other family has managed to get a tracking mark on her.  In less than a half hour the other party shows up, the young man Kyle and two guards arrive and arrogantly demand the lives of both of them.  

John knocks them unconscious with a thought and seals their cultivation.  Heather asks why, John states that she was polite and did not demand anything from him, Kyle was brash and stupid.

He allows Heather to continue on with her journey, telling her to get to her family and warns her of the tracking mark.  John sits and waits for the remainder of Kyles party to arrive.
