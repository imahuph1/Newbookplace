# outline

## born in a High martial world

Jim is an average student, sits around the middle of the class.  not a good fighter, ok in general studies.  nothing about him brings him to anyone's attention, he simply is.
He is not a hanger on of anyone.  Does not add his voice to the various conversations.
In general Jim is the average person.  With no obvious talents he does not work hard, does the bare minimum to pass.  He knows he is destined to work a customer service job post graduations.  He is ok with it.  The last thing he wants to do is stand out.  Standing out brings conflict, Jim hate conflict.

### Fallout

Schools begin to look into him, normal kid, both parents awakened nothing, family poor, no backing.  
Bringing him into school could be a genius student, good be a money pit.  How much to train six attributes.  how many resources to raise someone with six attributes cultivation.  to much of an unknown.

## Waking up

Jim wake up in the hospital, while he was unconscious the millions of years of cultivation practice have been fully digested, the various martial and cultivation techniques have been fully digested, the millions of years of combat have been fully digested, it took six days.  The healers had no idea what was wrong with him, perhaps the 6 attributes awakening all at once over stressed the body.

## Required collage entrance exam

This exam combines general knowledge, cultivation level, and a physical test.  Every student is required to participate even if they did not awaken an attribute.
Exam is three weeks following awakening day.  During these three weeks Jim attempts to come to terms with his overwhelming potential.  Every breath he takes is the equivalent of 3 hours of a genius cultivating.  His strength is growing rapidly, he is already at the third realm of nascent soul.  He is not taking any elixirs, it is all natural growth.  His parents, being average people, are proud to have a son such as him.  They know if his potential is tapped early retirement is in their future.
They are also worried for him, they know in the future he will be fighting dangerous beasts and delving nascent soul dangerous places for the good of what is left of humanity.

## Jim Holds Back

Jim is able to hide is aura, showing only 4 rank of body refinement, performs average on written test, hits and runs at the 4 rank of body refinement.  A few collage recruiters notice something is off about him but cannot put their fingers on it.  One thing of note, even without a strong backing and money to buy various elixirs Jim is still in the top 50 of high school graduates nation wide.
