# Vanderoth

## MC John Smith

50 something years old, retired electrician, fixed income, enjoys op mc web novel audiobooks.
Sits down and wrights his own wishlist if he where transmigrated. 

- infinite storage space, time locked, don't want to carry all my stuff around
- a few slots of infinite duping, don't like scarcity, if I find it I want all of it.
- in a rpg type world I would want a character trainer, no need to risk my life for small gains, just open the panel and add points.

With this wish list written, John fell asleep.  Little did he know that this was his last day on earth.

## Chat with a god

welcome, discuss wishlist, apply limits

infinite storage, impossible, but a large storage space, sure, John will be hard pressed to fill it up

infinite doubling, impossible, 10 slots, can double anything but money sure, anything doubled can only be used by John, sure.  Not killing the economy for John.

Character trainer, sure, can edit one item in the attribute and skills section for each level.

in exchange for these cheats, John gets a poor start, no family, no clan, no guild, start as a street beggar.

## waking up

It all seams like a really long dream, the chat with a god, his life as an orphan, leaving the orphanage, life on the street.  The stories of drunken adventurers he pickpocket.  It was a weird but wonderful dream.  That is until he got kicked in the ribs by a pointed shoe.
