# The wrath of a quiet man

Reborn as an orphan, goes to school each day, works and lives at an internet cafe after school, showers in a gym, washes cloths at a laundry, has about 5 days worth of clothing to his name. 

Awakening day comes, awakens the weakest possible spiritual root.  Completely useless.

John continues to live his life, no school any more, continues to live and work at the internet cafe, gets a daytime job to kill time, buy better cloths and food.

Jouhatsu not by choice but by circumstance.  In a world where the spiritual root basically determines your chance of moving up.  A low ranked spiritual root means that even if you do cultivate up your speed and max cultivation will forever be inferior to those who have higher level spiritual roots. 

A voice in his head offers him power beyond what can be reached on this planet, all he has to do is live, every day he is alive he will gain years of cultivation, skills, battle experience, as if he is living another life doubled each day, for up to one year. If he fights his cultivation will lock in at that point, if he goes an entire year without fighting he will be the most powerful being in this universe.  years of cultivation of 2<sup>364</sup> factorial.

Every day is a challenge, the voice nags him to fight, his cultivation stays at basically zero but with each day passing he could fight and gain the cultivation, after a month the temptation rises.

voice is an agent of chaos, just looking to have some fun, wants to see the world burn and john is a good catalyst for it.
