# World Rules

## Attributes

Max attribute 100 times tier.

level 1-10 100
level 11-20 200
level 21-30 300

attributes 

- Strength — physical power and likely melee damage/carrying capacity.
- Constitution — health, stamina, durability, and resistance.
- Dexterity — speed, reflexes, accuracy, and potentially attack speed.
- Wisdom - magical power, mental resistance, and related abilities.
- Spirit —  mana, mana recovery, and related abilities.

## Skills

gained from skill scrolls
skill scrolls split each ten levels
level 10 skill scroll opens up to 5 skills from 1-10
level 20 skill scroll opens up to 3 skills from 11-20
level 30 skill scroll opens up to 2 skills from 21-30
Skill crystals required to level up skills
Skill crystals ranked same as skill scrolls
the more skills opened the lower the chance for a skill scroll to succeed
special skill scrolls can be found in epic dungeons on the 10's guarantee opening a skill if one is available

## classes

ranked 1 star to 10 star
1 star class on free attribute point per level
10 star class 10 free attribute points per level

## choke points

To level up from 10 to 11, 20 to 21, ect must undergo a system defined quest, could be anything, some people get stuck at these choke points for years, if not their entire life.

## attribute crystals

found in dungeons, usually in epic mode, on the 10's
very rare, market but no supply,
generally used as soon as found
grants one free attribute point on use.

## Dungeons

can enter any dungeon that is equal to or above your level.
each dungeon has easy, medium, normal, and hard
dungeons on the 10's have epic mode
epic mode very difficult, cannot leave until dungeon is cleared

## two types of power

Might makes right, the stronger the person and the backing the more free to do anything a person is.

Money and political power.  The more money the person or family has the more free to do anything a person is.

a person without backing effectively must bow to a person with backing.
