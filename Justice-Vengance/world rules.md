# World Rules

## mana/magic

Mana and magic are common in this world, the vast majority of the population can use magic at its most basic level.  
There is a small percentage of the population that have strong enough mana sense/mana core to use higher levels of magic/perform stronger spells
There is a smaller percentage of the population that can awaken and truly utilize mana at the highest levels.
Among the awakened there are a few who merge with mana, become effectively immortal, matter and energy have become one.
