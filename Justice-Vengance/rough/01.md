# Fates Disturbance

Mike had an average day.  Fixing machines that are twenty years overdue on replacement, but it is still somehow cheeper to fix them than to replace them.  Not that he complains to much, it is a steady paycheck and the work is not to difficult.  
Per the norm nothing good on the evening news, corrupt politicians calling out corrupt CEO's, the CEO's striking back just as hard.  Nothing will be done about any of it, the fighting is just a floor show, keeping the people entertained.  
Even though he knows this, it still makes him mad.  The corruption has gotten so deep that the entire country, governmental system, and corporation needs to be torn down and rebuilt from scratch.  The joy of humanity, they are innately corrupt.  Mike on the other hand is just a small tooth on a cog in the machine, even if he quits and starts fighting back nobody would notice and he would be devoured by the machine.
A couple of hours and a few drinks later Mike turned the channel done with the repetitive twenty four hour news cycle.  He turned on some lowest common denominator situation comedy and drifted off into his own thoughts.  He dreamed of having the power to bring justice to those rich people who can somehow get away with everything, the worst thing to happen to them is maybe a small fine.  
Leaning back in his recliner he drained his last drink, he had the day off tomorrow, it is his fiftieth birthday and he was going to sleep in and relax.
