# Fates Disturbance

Mike had an average day.  Fixing machines that are twenty years overdue on replacement, but it is still somehow cheeper to fix them than to replace them.  Not that he complains to much, it is a steady paycheck and the work is not to difficult.  
Per the norm nothing good on the evening news, corrupt politicianse calling out corrupt CEO's, the CEO's striking back just as hard.  Nothing will be done about any of it, the fighting is just a floor show, keeping the people entertained.  
Even though he knows this, it still makes him mad.  The corruption has gotten so deap that the entire country, govermental system, and corperation needs to be torn down and rebuilt from scratch.  The joy of humanity, they are inately corrupt.  Mike on the other hand is just a small tooth on a cog in the machine, even if he quits and starts fighting back nobody would notice and he would be devoured by the machine.
