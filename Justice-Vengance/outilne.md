# Basic outline

## Book one

### rebirth arc

- Rebirth
  - born on earth, US, English speaker, dies peacefully at 50
  - reborn on Aezidore, six-year-old body, beaten and bruised, in a forest, no knowledge how got there, knows nothing about this world.
- Interlude
  - Incarnation of fate notice a strange disturbance in the ether
  - Sees two distinct futures for the kingdom, one of growth the other of destruction
  - call letting the other incarnations know what she saw
- Finding mage tower
  - stumbles into hunting cabin, door opens, falls inside
  - wake up some time later in a bed, thin soup on table nearby
  - the orbs in the cabin teach him the common language over time
- Learning Magical Language
  - written it is an enchantment or magical circle
  - can be spoken or drawn in the air with a staff, finger, wand
  - when spoken and drawn faster
- learning elements
  - Light, healing
  - Darkness, destruction
  - Air, includes lightning if compressed enough, things far apart
  - Earth, opposite of air, things close together
  - Water, condense water, make ice, slow things down
  - Fire, speed things up
- meeting first mentor
  - fight beast in forest for game
  - speak to each other, find common ground, learn about balance in forest
  - not all magical beasts bad just like not all people bad
  - beast is a natural mage, a magical beast, becomes mentor
- Enlightenment
  - closer to the world flow, no longer need to speak or draw runes
- meeting first noble advocate/sponsor
  - going to small village, set up small healer shop, now age 8
  - during festival meet local baron, he is interested in you having a strong mage on his side is good for a low level noble
  - sponsors you to academy
  - you decide to go to learn modern magic, the spells you use are hundreds of years old.  you want to see what progress has been made

### academy arc

- Going to academy age 12
  - pass entrance exams
  - due to his skill and past practice mike joins year three of the academy 2 years younger than the other students in the class, learning more complex spells, dimensional magic, enchanting, potion making
  - meet headmaster
  - family name/background not supposed to matter
  - receive silent witness, will record interactions when turned on
  - most do not use it, wealthy powerful students have stated it is a sign of weakness
  - you do not care what teenagers think of you
- first arrogant nobles
  - after first class, approached and mocked due to not having a family name
  - threatened if he does not kneel before his betters
  - smart ass remarks lead to nobles starting fight
  - Mike does not strike back, takes a beating, turns the nobles in, they get expelled.
- Ostracized due to using the silent witness
